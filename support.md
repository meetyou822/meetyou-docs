# Support

**Email:** meetyou.acc1@gmail.com

Write to us for any of the following:

- A question about how the app works.
- A bug report — please include your device, iOS or Android version, and
  what you were doing when it happened.
- A safety concern about another user, an event, or a photo. You can also
  report or block directly from inside the app: open the event in question
  and use the flag icon (Report) or the block icon next to it.
- A request about your data — a copy, a correction, or deletion. You can
  also delete your account yourself at any time from Settings → Delete
  Account.

We read every message sent to this address.
