## 1. Our standard

MeetYou has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**.
This applies to every part of the app: event listings, photographs, ratings
and comments, profile content, and any message sent through a report.

MeetYou is intended for people aged **16 and over** (see the
[Privacy Policy §10](privacy-policy.md#10-age) and
[Terms of Use §2](terms-of-use.md)). ⚠️ As those documents already disclose,
nothing in the app currently verifies a user's age at sign-up — this page
does not change that; it describes what happens once a concern is raised,
regardless of how old anyone involved turns out to be.

## 2. How to report a concern

Every event, photo, profile and person in the app can be reported from inside
the app itself — look for the flag icon and choose **"Safety concern"** as the
reason. Reports can also be sent directly to **meetyou.acc1@gmail.com**.

A report reaches the operator only — not the person it's about — so it can be
acted on before anyone is tipped off. See the Privacy Policy's
[§9](privacy-policy.md#9-reports-and-moderation) for how reports are handled
and retained.

## 3. What happens after a report

Every report is reviewed by a human operator. For anything involving CSAE, the
content is removed immediately and the responsible account is disabled — the
same power already described in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for
moderation generally, applied here with the highest priority.

Where the law requires it, we report CSAE content or activity to the
appropriate law enforcement and national authorities. MeetYou operates under
Czech and EU law; reports involving illegal content are escalated to the
relevant national hotline or law enforcement body rather than handled
internally.
